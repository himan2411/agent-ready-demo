Larkline

Larkline is a fictional invoicing software company for small teams, with recurring invoices and two accounting integrations. It exists only as a demo.

Questions

Is Larkline a real company?
No. Larkline is a fictional company and every fact on this site is made up. It is a demo of an agent-ready business by Himanshu Surana.
How much does Larkline cost?
Plans start at $19 a month. Solo is $19 a month for one user. Team is $49 a month for up to 50 seats.
Does Larkline work with accounting tools?
Yes. Two accounting integrations ship today: Xero and QuickBooks Online.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. 14 days on any plan, no card needed.
Can an AI agent book a Larkline demo?
An agent can check services, open demo slots and policies through a read-only MCP server. It cannot book or pay. The buyer books by email.
